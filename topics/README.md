# RESONANCE Topics

RESONANCE organizes coverage around five connected domains rather than traditional newsroom desks.

## AI

Models, agents, robotics, compute, inference, memory, orchestration, developer tools, autonomous systems and intelligence infrastructure.

Key question: **What new capability has become possible, and what system does it depend on?**

## Science

Research, discovery, reproducibility, scientific AI, laboratories, biotechnology, physics, space, climate, scientific institutions and research infrastructure.

Key question: **What is the evidence, and can the result be independently examined or reproduced?**

## Startups & Economy

Companies, markets, capital, labor, business models, platform shifts, new industries and economic consequences of technological change.

Key question: **Where is value moving, why, and who captures it?**

## Trust & Verification

AI safety, security, provenance, identity, permissions, governance, observability, evaluation, recovery, evidence and institutional trust.

Key question: **How do we know the system did what it was supposed to do?**

## Human

Cognition, learning, creativity, flow, attention, health, work, relationships, meaning and adaptation to intelligent systems.

Key question: **What does technological change do to human capability and human life?**

## Cross-topic stories

The most important stories will often belong to several topics at once.

For example:

```text
AI agent
   ↓
new technical capability
   ↓
new startup market
   ↓
new trust requirement
   ↓
new form of human work
```

RESONANCE should follow the causal chain rather than force a story into a single category.
