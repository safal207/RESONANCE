# 60-second demonstration script

**0–15 seconds — Exactness.** Open “A different commit”. A real successful check supports
its exact PR-head commit. Move the control to the merge commit: the answer becomes
UNKNOWN. We are not claiming the merge failed; the evidence does not establish it.

**15–30 seconds — Time.** Open “Before we knew”. Move back before local receipt. A
historical source result must not be silently inserted into what the system knew then.

**30–50 seconds — Revision.** Open “A bad reason to revise”. Move forward. The original
policy erases valid support on an expired diagnostic; the candidate validates that
dependency. This is a labeled synthetic reproducer, not a GitHub incident.

**50–60 seconds — Bounded ask.** We are looking for a design partner with one read-only
workflow and an independent engineer who will reproduce or challenge one case.
We are not presenting an LLM leaderboard, a production certification or revenue traction.

Proposed one-sentence positioning: “An evidence-aware review layer that explains
whether a changed conclusion is justified for this version, at this time.”

This is draft positioning for the working prototype, not a validated market claim.
