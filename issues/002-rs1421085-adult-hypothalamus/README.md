# RESONANCE Issue 002 — The hidden adult state of an obesity-risk locus

## Editorial question

What regulatory state exists in the adult hypothalamus at the obesity-associated `rs1421085` locus between the exact DNA substitution and the known increase in `Irx3`?

## Lead finding

A provenance-first reanalysis of two independent public adult-mouse hypothalamus datasets identifies a narrow computational discovery candidate:

> The 500-bp interval containing the exact mouse ortholog of human `rs1421085` shows rare, subthreshold chromatin accessibility with reproducible neuronal subclass structure, and an independent paired Multiome dataset contains rare nuclei in which accessibility at the same interval co-occurs with `Irx3` transcription.

This is **not** a claim that the variant causes the accessibility state, that the interval is an active enhancer in those cells, or that the mediator has been identified.

## Discovery level

- Level 1 — computational discovery candidate: **PASS**
- Level 2 — mechanistic computational evidence: **NOT YET**
- Level 3 — causal molecular mechanism: **NOT YET**
