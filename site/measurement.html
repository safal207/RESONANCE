Measure the path. Do not identify the reader.

RESONANCE wants to know whether an article reaches its hot question and creates a real workflow conversation. It does not need a persistent reader profile to answer that question.

RESONANCE Privacy-Aware Analytics v0.7

What we measure

Article→Meaningful read→Hot question→Workflow intake→Market evidence

A meaningful read means an article remained visible for at least 45 seconds and reached at least 60% scroll depth during the same page load. A hot-question view means the market question became materially visible. A workflow-intake open means the reader activated the public GitHub workflow form.

What we do not measure

Privacy signals are authoritative

If Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is enabled, the runtime does not emit analytics events. Automated browser audits are also excluded so Site Health does not manufacture its own readership.

Transport is default-off

The journal only sends events when an HTTPS collector endpoint is explicitly configured during the publication build. Requests omit credentials and referrer information. Until a collector passes a separate provider/retention/privacy review, the runtime remains measurement-ready but transport-disabled.

Analytics is not demand evidence

Reading and clicking are useful behavioral signals, but they do not prove a real workflow, a real failure, willingness to test, product-market fit or revenue. Those claims begin only when a person submits a generalized workflow and it enters the existing Problem Card / Product Signal / Demand Graph process.
