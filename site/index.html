Verified Report #023 · Result handoff / stale work salvage

The work survived. The authority did not.

When that stale result auto-published, A/fence 1 and current owner B/fence 2 both published the same digest, creating two effects. In the safe path, artifact production created zero effects; B explicitly adopted the exact digest under its current epoch, committed once, and stale A received 409 / fenced_out. Score: 10/10.
