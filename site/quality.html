Measure the publication, not just the content.

RESONANCE treats the website itself as a verifiable system: structure is checked before release, browser experience is measured in CI, and the deployed GitHub Pages site is measured again after publication.

RESONANCE Site Health v0.1

1. Publication Contract

The deterministic publication contract builds the journal and checks canonical URLs, structured data, multilingual metadata, sitemap coverage, Market OS evidence boundaries and other release invariants before deployment.

2. Experience Contract

Pull requests that change the publication run Lighthouse against a local build. Representative pages are measured on mobile, with key pages also measured on desktop.

3. Live Experience Audit

After a successful GitHub Pages deployment, Lighthouse measures the public site again. The result is stored as an immutable workflow artifact with the audited commit/run context.

Evidence produced

Readable summary

Raw reports

Budgets

Performance, accessibility, best-practice and SEO thresholds are versioned in the repository.

What the numbers do not prove

Lighthouse is controlled lab evidence. A high score does not prove Google ranking, real-user Core Web Vitals, conversion, or identical performance on every device and network. Those claims require their own evidence.
