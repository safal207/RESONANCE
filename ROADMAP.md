## v1.0 — RESONANCE

**Goal:** a global evidence-first journal and market-learning system for intelligence, technology, science, trust and human progress.

A mature RESONANCE story should be readable at three levels:

1. **Reader:** What happened and why does it matter?
2. **Expert:** Is the reasoning technically sound?
3. **Verifier:** Can I inspect the claims, evidence, uncertainty and changes over time?

Selected market-learning stories add a fourth level:

4. **Builder / client:** What real workflow remains unresolved, what guarantee is missing, and what would prove the problem solved?

## Product principle

The website is not the product by itself.

The durable asset is:

**information + reasoning + evidence + verification + market dialogue + validated demand + reusable capability.**

RESONANCE should not confuse attention with demand, synthetic examples with customers, or one custom request with a scalable product.
